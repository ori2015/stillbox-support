Stillbox Support

Stillbox keeps your photos, videos and files in an encrypted vault that lives in a folder you choose. No account, no tracking, no developer servers — your keys stay on your devices.

Contact: GitHub Issues · Privacy policy: privacy.html

Troubleshooting

I can't unlock my vault

  1. Wrong PIN? After several wrong attempts the app deliberately waits longer each time before accepting another try. Wait for the countdown, then try again carefully. The delay is remembered, so closing and reopening the app does not shorten it; a correct unlock clears it.
  2. Face ID not appearing or failing? Make sure Face ID is enrolled and allowed for the app in iOS Settings → Face ID & Passcode → Other Apps. Then return to the app and try again.
  3. Re-enrolled Face ID recently (or restored the phone)? The old device-bound secret is invalidated by design when biometrics change. Use your 24-word recovery phrase to open the vault, then set up a new PIN.
  4. Opened on a new device? Use the recovery phrase — that is exactly what it is for.

My iCloud vault opens slowly or shows "waiting"

Parts of the folder may not be downloaded yet. The app asks iCloud to fetch them before it can read; keep the app open, connected to Wi-Fi and power, and give it time for large vaults. Once materialized locally, opening is fast again. If it stays stuck: open the Files app, navigate to the vault's parent folder, and confirm files show either downloaded or "downloading" status; pause other large syncs if needed.

The app locked itself while I was using it

That is the intended behavior: leaving the app (or certain system events) locks the vault immediately and covers the screen in the app switcher. Unlock again with Face ID + PIN.

An import or export was interrupted

Nothing needs cleanup: imports write encrypted chunks before they are recorded in the index, so a failed import leaves no half item behind (any unused data is cleaned up automatically later). A cancelled export discards its temporary file when the share sheet closes.

I moved or renamed my vault folder in Files

Move it back / rename it back to what the app expects, or remove it with Forget saved folder and re-add the folder from its new location. The vault contents are unaffected by being moved.

Moving to a new iPhone

  1. Make sure your vault folder is reachable on the new device (iCloud Drive, or transfer via Files/AirDrop/backup-restore of On My iPhone).
  2. Install the app, choose Open existing vault, select the .vault folder.
  3. Unlock with your 24-word recovery phrase, then set a PIN for that device.

Something else went wrong

Open an issue at https://github.com/ori2015/stillbox-support/issues/new with: what you did, what you expected, what happened, device model and iOS version. Optionally open Switch vault → Export support diagnostics and paste the result. Please never send your recovery phrase, PIN, or vault contents.

Frequently asked questions

The complete FAQ is below; these are the most common ones.

Where are my files stored? In a folder you chose (Files / iCloud Drive). Encrypted on your device first.

Can you see my photos? No. There is no server, no account, no way in — for us or anyone else.

Do you upload anything? No analytics, no telemetry, no uploads. The free version shows non-personalised ads (nothing about your vault is shared with the ad SDK); one purchase removes them. Only your own storage provider holds copies of the encrypted folder, under your own account.

I lost my recovery phrase. If the device route is also gone, the vault cannot be opened by anyone. This is permanent by design — there is no reset.

I deleted an item inside the vault. Can I undo it? Not in this version; removal is permanent. Originals outside the vault were never touched unless you explicitly chose delete-after-import.

Frequently asked questions

What exactly is a "vault"?

A vault is an ordinary folder whose contents have been encrypted. It has a name like MyVault.vault and lives wherever folders live on your iPhone. Inside, your photos, videos and files are stored in encrypted form — along with their names, thumbnails and albums — so nothing about them can be read without your key. You can copy that folder to another device or storage service and it will open there with your recovery phrase. The vault belongs to you, like a document you own, not an account you sign into.

Where are my files actually stored?

In a folder you choose through the iOS Files picker — On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, or another location Files offers. The app reads and writes only that folder. There is no Stillbox server involved at any point; if you pick iCloud Drive, Apple holds the (encrypted) folder under your own Apple ID.

Can the developer see my photos or videos?

No. Encryption happens on your device before anything is written, and decryption happens on your device when you view something. Your keys are derived from secrets that never leave your phone. We have no server, no account system, and no way to access your vault — technically, not just by policy.

Do you upload anything anywhere?

No. The app has no servers of ours, no analytics, no crash reporting and no telemetry. The free version shows non-personalised Google AdMob ads (nothing about your vault is ever given to them), and a one-time purchase removes them. The only time anything leaves your device is when you yourself export or share a file, or when your chosen storage provider (e.g. iCloud) syncs the encrypted folder under your account.

I lost my recovery phrase. Can you restore my vault?

Unfortunately no — and we want to be direct about this rather than soften it. If you have lost both the device unlock route (Face ID + PIN) and the recovery phrase, the data inside the vault cannot be decrypted by anyone, including us. That is permanent by design: the app has no back door, because a back door would break the privacy the app exists to provide. Save the phrase somewhere safe — a password manager, printed paper in a secure place — the moment you create a vault.

I reset Face ID / changed my Face ID enrollment and now the app won't open

The Face ID step protects a device-local, non-synchronizing Keychain secret tied to the current biometric enrollment. When you re-enroll Face ID, that secret is invalidated by iOS — deliberately, so a stolen phone with new fingers can't be used. Your vault itself is untouched. Open the app on the same vault using your 24-word recovery phrase, then set up a new PIN; a fresh device unlock route is created and your files and albums stay as they were.

What does "Forget saved folder" delete — and what doesn't it?

Forget removes the app's memory of your vault, from the device:

  • the security-scoped bookmark (access grant) to your folder,
  • the device-bound unlock credential for that vault,
  • the local offline cache kept on the device,
  • the saved-folder registry entries.

It does not delete your actual vault folder. The .vault folder with all its encrypted files remains exactly where you put it — in Files, iCloud Drive, etc. You can delete it manually like any file, or re-add it later with your 24-word recovery phrase. Forget removes the local Face ID/PIN credential, so that old PIN route is intentionally no longer available.

I deleted a photo from the vault. Can I get it back?

Not in this version, and we say so plainly: removing an item from a vault is permanent — there is no Recently Deleted bin. The original file outside the vault (if one still exists where you imported it from) is never touched by removing the item from the vault. If you might regret a deletion, export the item first.

Why does a vault stored in iCloud open slowly the first time?

When a vault lives in iCloud Drive, some of its files may exist in the cloud but not yet on your iPhone. Before reading, the app asks iOS to download ("materialize") those pieces. Depending on size and connection, first open can take a little while; afterwards, opening is fast because the data is local. Keep the app open and connected while it finishes.

How does the offline cache work?

The app keeps a small, private, on-device cache of recently used data so browsing and playback stay quick:

  • It stores encrypted data only — recently viewed media chunks, encrypted index generations and thumbnails — verified against checksums before use.
  • The cache is capped (well below your free space) and least-recently-used items fall out automatically.
  • Items marked Keep offline get a dedicated portion so they stay available in airplane mode.
  • Nothing that could unlock your vault is ever cached: no PIN material, no keys, and no unencrypted media. The cache is excluded from backups and is removed with Forget saved folder or by deleting the app. Use Forget first if you also want the device-local Keychain credential gone: iOS keeps Keychain items after an app is deleted.

How do I export diagnostics?

Open Switch vault and tap Export support diagnostics. It copies a sanitized JSON document (event codes only: no file names, paths, keys, PINs or recovery phrases) to the clipboard; you decide whether to paste it into a support message. Nothing is sent automatically, and the app has no upload service.

How do I contact support?

Open an issue at github.com/ori2015/stillbox-support/issues/new. Describe what you did and what happened; include a screenshot if it helps. Please never email us your recovery phrase, your PIN, or files from your vault — we don't need them to help, and sending them would put your data at risk.
