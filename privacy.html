Privacy policy — Stillbox

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Stillbox ("the app", "we") is built so that we cannot see your data. This policy explains exactly what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version

Your vault content never leaves your device, and we collect nothing. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob; a one-time purchase, Remove ads, turns them off. Details under "Advertising" below.

  • No accounts. The app has no sign-up, no user profile, and no user ids.
  • No analytics or telemetry. The app contains no analytics SDKs and sends no usage statistics, crash reports to us, or performance metrics anywhere.
  • No tracking. No cross-app or cross-device tracking of any kind; the app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission and does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • No developer servers. The app has no backend of ours. The only network traffic is the ad SDK's requests to Google (see "Advertising") and the App Store purchase; there is no server of ours that could receive your files, metadata, or device information.

Advertising

The free version shows banner ads at the top and bottom of the screen and, rarely, a full-screen ad after you lock the vault. They are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver an ad, Google receives the device's IP address and technical device data and acts under the Google Privacy Policy. Nothing about your vault — file names, contents, albums, keys, PIN or recovery phrase — is ever given to the ad SDK, and ads are not personalised. Because ads run in the same app, a banner can be visible while your vault is open; it never has access to what is on screen. In the EEA and UK a Google consent form is shown first and can be reopened under Privacy choices on the home screen. The one-time purchase Remove ads (processed by Apple) turns all ads off; Apple, not us, receives the payment details.

Where your data lives

Your vault is a folder you choose — for example On My iPhone, iCloud Drive, or another storage location available through the iOS Files app. All encryption and decryption happens on your device. What gets written to that folder is ciphertext: file contents, names, thumbnails, and album structure are all encrypted before they leave the app's memory. The folder also contains a small plaintext header describing key-derivation parameters (including algorithm settings, format/version identifiers, timestamps, chunking parameters and encrypted key-slot metadata; an optional device label may also appear). It contains no plaintext PIN, recovery phrase, encryption key, filename, album name or media content.

Storage providers act independently of us. If you place your vault in iCloud Drive or another provider's storage, that provider stores and syncs those files under your account with them, under their terms and privacy policy. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's own privacy policy; we have no visibility into it and no relationship with your provider. The provider sees encrypted content objects plus the non-secret plaintext format header described above — but you should choose providers whose terms you trust.

Information the app keeps on your device

The following never leaves your device unless you move it:

  • Device credential: a device-local, non-synchronizing Keychain secret, protected by Face ID and combined with your local PIN to unlock the vault. A device passcode must be configured for Face ID, but the app deliberately offers no passcode fallback.
  • Unlock-attempt counter: a small device-local, non-synchronizing Keychain item that records how many wrong PINs were entered in a row and until when further attempts are delayed. It contains no PIN, key or vault data, and it is cleared by a successful unlock. It exists so that closing or reinstalling the app does not reset the delay.
  • Local cache: an offline cache of recently used encrypted data, kept in app-private storage excluded from backups, to make browsing fast. It can be removed via Forget saved folder or by deleting the app.
  • Security-scoped bookmark: a reference letting the app reach the vault folder you selected. Removed via Forget saved folder.

Deleting the app. iOS keeps Keychain items on the device after an app is deleted. To remove the device credential and the other items above, use Forget saved folder in the app before deleting it (or erase the device). The encrypted vault folder itself is never deleted by the app.

Recovery phrase

Your 24-word recovery phrase is generated on your device and shown once. We never receive it and cannot recover it for you. If you tap Copy recovery phrase, the phrase is placed on the iOS pasteboard as a device-local item that iOS removes automatically after about a minute; it is not shared with your other devices through Universal Clipboard. Pasteboard contents are visible to other apps you use while the item is present, so we recommend writing the phrase down instead. If you lose both device access and the recovery phrase, the data in your vault cannot be decrypted — by anyone, including us. There is no back door.

Diagnostics

The app keeps a short, in-memory log of technical event codes (for example "an import failed") that disappears when the app closes. If you ask for help, you can choose Switch vault → Export support diagnostics to copy a sanitized JSON report (no file names, paths, keys, PINs or recovery phrases) to the clipboard and paste it into a support message. Nothing diagnostic is ever uploaded automatically. Builds made for development also contain a local self-test; it is not part of the App Store version.

Support communication

If you contact us through GitHub Issues, we receive what you send (typically your message and contact details) and use it only to respond and improve the app. Please never send recovery phrases, PINs, or vault contents.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction), does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone — including children — because we collect no personal information ourselves, and contains no user-generated public content. The ad SDK is configured for non-personalised ads only.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes in ways that matter to this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Because the app has no update channel of its own, material changes will also be reflected in the App Store release notes. The version of this policy that applied when you obtained the app remains archived at https://ori2015.github.io/stillbox-support/privacy.html.
